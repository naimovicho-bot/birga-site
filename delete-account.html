Deleting your BIRGA account

Updated: 2026-10-10

How to delete your account

Open the BIRGA app and sign in.

Go to Profile → Delete account and confirm.

The account is deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

What is deleted

Phone number, email address, name, age, gender, photo, city, languages, interests, bio, saved listings, blocks, and reviews about you. Upcoming trips and activities are unpublished.

What is kept

Messages you sent to other people and reviews you wrote about others are kept without your name. Reports may be kept for safety purposes.

If you cannot sign in

Write to us from the phone number or email address linked to the account and we will delete it.
