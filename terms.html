Terms of Use

Updated: 2026-10-10

What BIRGA is

BIRGA is a place where people find companions for trips and shared activities. The operator is BIRGA.

BIRGA is not a tour operator, carrier or trip organiser, does not sell tours and does not verify users' identity. The “Photo checked” badge only means a moderator compared the profile photo with a live photo of that person; name, age and documents are not checked.

Who can use it

You must be at least 16 years old. Give true information about yourself and do not create an account in someone else's name.

Rules of conduct

Not allowed: insults, threats, harassment and discrimination; fraud and requests for advance payment; fake profiles; spam and advertising; illegal content and sexual content; collecting other people's personal data.

We have zero tolerance for such content and behaviour.

Reports and blocks

You can report a user and block them from their profile. After a block they cannot message you.

We review reports within 24 hours and may remove content or close the offender's account without notice.

Your content

Everything you post stays yours. You allow us to show it to other users inside the app. Do not post anything you have no right to post.

Meeting safely

You decide who to meet and travel with, and you do so at your own risk. Meet in public places, tell people close to you about your plans, and do not send money to strangers.

In an emergency call 112.

Liability

The app is provided “as is”. We are not responsible for users' actions, for how trips and meetings go, or for arrangements between users.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time: Profile → Delete account.

Changes

We may update these terms. By continuing to use the app you accept the new version.
