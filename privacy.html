Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-10-10

Who we are

BIRGA helps people find companions for trips and shared activities. The operator of the app and of your data is BIRGA.

What data we collect

To sign in: your phone number or your email address.

Profile: name, age, gender, photo, city, languages, interests, travel style and bio — what you enter yourself.

Your activity in the app: trips and activities you create or join, saved listings, reviews and ratings, reports and blocks.

Chat messages, including photos and voice messages.

Photo check (only if you choose to take it): one camera photo with a given gesture. Only moderators see it, it is never shown on your profile, and it is deleted as soon as the moderator decides, and no later than 7 days. After that only the result and the date are kept.

Technical data: IP address — to prevent abuse and limit sign-in attempts.

We do not collect your device location, your contact list or payment data. The app has no ads and no advertising trackers.

Why we use it

So you can sign in, and so nobody else can sign in as you.

To show your profile and listings to other users and match companions.

To run chats, reviews, reports and blocks.

To run the AI assistant (see below).

What other users see

Name, age, gender, photo, city, languages, interests, bio, rating and reviews, and your trips and activities.

Your phone number and email address are never shown to anyone.

Who we share data with

The SMS service and the email service — only the number or address, to deliver your sign-in code.

The hosting provider whose servers run the app and the file storage.

AI assistant: the text of your request and public data about trips, activities and profiles (name, city, interests) are processed by the Google Gemini model. Phone numbers, email addresses and chat messages are not sent to the AI.

We do not sell your data. Data is given to public authorities only on a lawful request.

How long we keep data

For as long as your account exists. Sign-in codes are valid for a few minutes and we never store them in readable form.

After you delete your account — see “Deleting your account”.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account. It happens immediately.

Deleted: phone number, email address, name, age, gender, photo, city, languages, interests, bio, saved listings, blocks, and reviews about you. Your upcoming trips and activities are unpublished and you are removed from other people's.

Kept without your name: messages you sent to other people (they stay with those people) and reviews you wrote about others. Reports may be kept for safety purposes.

Your rights

You can view and change your profile, add a second sign-in method and delete your account at any time. For questions about your data, write to us.

Age

The app is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will appear in the app with a new update date.
